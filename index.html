Wand Search!

Are you able to find Wendy's magic wand before the aliens take over the world?

It was 6:00 AM and Wendy was eating breakfast until a portal appeared and a letter came out. It read "In 5 days, aliens from outer space will come to earth and take over the world." In panic, Wendy goes to get her magic wand only to realize she left it at school!

There are 5 locations

  • The classroom
  • The caferteria
  • The principl's office
  • Outside of the school
Wendy can only go to one location per day so choose wisely
