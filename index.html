Wand Search!

Are you able to find Wendy's magic wand before the aliens take over the world?

It was 6:00 AM and Wendy was eating breakfast until a portal appeared and a letter came out. It read "In 10 days, aliens from outer space will come to earth and take over the world." In panic, Wendy goes to get her magic wand only to realize she left it at school!

There are 5 locations

  • The hallway
  • The classroom
  • The cafeteria
  • The principal's office
  • Outside of the school

Wendy has 10 days to find her wand so choose wisely
